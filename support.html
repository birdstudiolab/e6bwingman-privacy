E6B Wingman — Support

Your data is yours. Your log lives as fleet.json in the app's Documents folder (visible in the Files app). Use Export in Settings to make a backup any time; use Import to bring it back. Getting your own file back never costs anything.

Frequently asked

The unlock didn't appear after I paid? Apple sometimes confirms a purchase a few minutes later (family approval, payment verification). E6B Wingman re-checks automatically and unlocks itself the moment the receipt lands. You are never charged twice for the same unlock. If it still doesn't land after a few minutes, use Settings → Restore.

Restoring on a new phone? Settings → Restore with the same Apple ID brings your unlock back.

Moving to another device or sharing with a buddy? Export a backup and import it on the other phone — both are free.

Units? Every calculator is free; one aircraft is free forever. Settings → Unlock removes the fleet limit and enables report sharing — one payment, no subscription.
