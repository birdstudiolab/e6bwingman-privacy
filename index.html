E6B Wingman — Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 7, 2026

E6B Wingman is a flight computer & weight & balance app for iPhone. It was built so that the honest answer to "what does this app collect?" is nothing.

What E6B Wingman collects

Nothing. E6B Wingman has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no third-party SDKs. The developer cannot see your flights, your name, or anything you type into the app.

Where your data lives

Your aircraft and weight & balance data are stored as a file (fleet.json) in the app's own Documents folder on your device. It never leaves your device unless you personally export it using the Export button and choose where to put it.

Network use

The app makes no network requests of its own. The only network interaction is with Apple's App Store, and only if you choose to buy the one-time unlock or restore a purchase. That interaction is handled by Apple's StoreKit under Apple's own privacy policy.

Permissions

E6B Wingman requests no system permissions (no location, no photos, no camera, no contacts).

Children

E6B Wingman is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will replace this page and carry a new effective date. The app itself will still collect nothing.
